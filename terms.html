2. Risk disclosure

  • Probabilities, backtests and expected values are estimates built from past prices. Past results do not predict future results, and a high percentage is not a guarantee.
  • Price data comes from a third party, can be delayed, incomplete or wrong, and may differ from the price your broker gives you.
  • Real trades cost money (spreads, commissions, slippage, financing). The cost setting in Signal Desk is only an estimate that you control.
  • Stops and targets are not guaranteed. Prices can gap past them, especially when a market is closed, around news and at earnings.
  • Market-hours and event warnings are best effort. Holidays and many scheduled releases are not checked, and the AI has no knowledge of live news.
  • Risking a large share of your account on one trade (the slider allows up to 75%) can wipe out your account quickly.
  • Paper trading uses pretend money. Its results are simulated, assume fills at the last close and ignore queueing, partial fills and market impact, so they are not a promise of what real trading would have produced.
  • Profit and loss figures in the app only reflect the trades and prices you entered or the tool recorded. They are not account statements and are not tax records.
